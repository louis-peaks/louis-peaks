## This website

Louis.Peaks is a personal website about endurance sport, mountains and outdoor experiences.

This version of the website does not include analytics scripts, advertising cookies, an account system, submission forms or embedded third-party media. The site code does not store visitor information in cookies or browser local storage.

## Hosting

The website is intended to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider handles the connection information needed to deliver pages, such as an IP address and technical request information. The provider’s handling of this information is described in its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## External links and contact

Links to Instagram, TikTok, Strava and other websites open services controlled by their respective providers. Their own privacy practices apply when you visit them.

If you contact Louis through social media or email, the service you use processes that message. A contact email address has not yet been supplied for this website.
