## Starting with curiosity

I came into triathlon with more confidence in running than in the other two disciplines. That is useful, but it can also be a trap. The current phase is about building skills, finding repeatable routines and discovering what sustainable effort feels like.

## What I want to remember

Swimming, cycling and running each ask a different question. I want to leave room to learn before assuming I know the answer.
